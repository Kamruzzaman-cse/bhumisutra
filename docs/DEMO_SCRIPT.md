# Demo video script (max 3:00)

Record in Chrome at 1366×768 or larger (OBS Studio, or Xbox Game Bar with Win+Alt+R). Speak in Bangla. Rehearse twice. Keep a real phone ready for the QR scan.

**Before recording:**
- Open the live link.
- Log in as `admin` → **Ledger → Restore demo data**, then log out.
- Keep the app in Bangla.

**Tips:**
- Hide bookmarks and notifications.
- Zoom the browser to 110%.

- Export as MP4, 1080p, under 3:00.
